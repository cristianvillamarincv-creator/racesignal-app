# RaceSignal Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [fill in publish date]_

RaceSignal ("RaceSignal," "we," "us") is an app that helps endurance athletes track their race
history and get AI-assisted analysis of their performance. This policy explains what information
RaceSignal collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it.

## Information we collect

**Account email.** When you sign in to RaceSignal, we collect your email address. We use it to
create and identify your account and to send you a one-time sign-in link. We don't use it for
marketing.

**Race and performance data.** RaceSignal stores the race results you add or import, including
event name, date, location, sport, finish time, splits, and ranking/placement information. Some of
this data is entered manually by you; some is imported from publicly available race-results pages
on third-party race-timing/results providers, which you confirm belong to you during setup.

**Signal conversations.** When you ask Signal (RaceSignal's AI analysis feature) a question, we
store the text of that conversation — your questions and Signal's responses — so you can return to
it later.

**Screenshots you attach to Signal.** If you attach a screenshot (for example, from Garmin,
Strava, TrainingPeaks, or another training app) to a Signal conversation as evidence for analysis,
that image is sent once, for that one message, directly to our AI provider for analysis. **We do
not store the image itself** — RaceSignal never saves it to our servers or to your account beyond
that one request. Only Signal's own written description of what it found in the image (as part of
its reply) is saved to your conversation history, so you can refer back to it later. The photo
library permission RaceSignal requests is used solely to let you pick that screenshot; RaceSignal
does not access your photo library for any other purpose.

## How we use and share information

**Supabase.** RaceSignal is built on Supabase, which hosts our database and handles
authentication. Your account and race data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure under our
control.

**Anthropic (Signal's AI provider).** Signal is powered by Anthropic's Claude models. To generate
an analysis, RaceSignal sends Anthropic the race data relevant to your question (drawn from the
race and performance data described above) and, if you've attached one, that turn's screenshot, so
it can generate a response. Anthropic processes this data to return that response; RaceSignal does
not control Anthropic's own data-handling practices beyond that request. You can read Anthropic's
privacy policy at https://www.anthropic.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not run
any analytics or advertising tracking in RaceSignal.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Screenshots are never retained past the one
request described above, regardless of what happens to your account.

**Deleting your account.** You can permanently delete your RaceSignal account at any time from
**Settings → Delete account**. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your race
history, your Signal conversations, and every other record tied to your account. This can't be
undone, and we don't keep a separate copy of it afterward beyond routine, short-lived
infrastructure backups used for disaster recovery.

## Your choices and contact

If you have questions about your data, want to request its deletion outside the in-app flow, or
need help with anything else, email us at **racesignal@gmail.com**.

## Children's privacy

RaceSignal isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children
under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

## Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Continued
use of RaceSignal after a change means you accept the updated policy.
