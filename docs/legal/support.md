# RaceSignal Support

**racesignal@gmail.com**

Have a question, found a bug, or need help with your account? Email us at the address above and
we'll get back to you.

## Reporting a problem

To help us help you faster, please include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead (a screenshot helps, if you can share one)
- Your account's email address
- Your device (e.g. iPhone 15) and, if you know it, your iOS version

## Account help and deletion

You can permanently delete your RaceSignal account at any time from **Settings → Delete account**
in the app — this immediately deletes your account and all associated data. If you'd rather
request deletion by email instead, or run into trouble with the in-app option, contact us at
racesignal@gmail.com.

## Subscription and billing support

RaceSignal subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID, not directly by us. To
view, change, or cancel a subscription, or to request a refund, use your device's **App Store
account settings** (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iPhone). For anything else related
to your subscription — like a question about what's included — email racesignal@gmail.com.
